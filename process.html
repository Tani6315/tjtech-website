Our process

From first call to live launch.

A proven 12-step roadmap that keeps your project clear, on time and on budget, with your approval at every key milestone.

Project journey map

Your project, step by step

Six phases, twelve milestones, here’s the full journey at a glance.

In detail

What happens at each stage

01
Phase 1 · Discover

Discovery Call

A free consultation to understand your business, goals, audience and the problem we’re solving together.

Deliverable: Project brief
02
Phase 1 · Discover

Client Data Collection

We gather everything needed: business details, logo & brand assets, content, photos, competitor references and any existing accounts.

Deliverable: Content & asset checklist
03
Phase 1 · Discover

Requirement Analysis

Features, pages, user roles and integrations are documented clearly so nothing is missed or misunderstood.

Deliverable: Requirements document
04
Phase 2 · Plan

Planning & Proposal

We define the sitemap, tech stack, milestones and timeline, and share a transparent quote for your approval.

Deliverable: Proposal, timeline & quote
05
Phase 2 · Plan

Wireframing & UX

Low-fidelity layouts map out structure and user flow before any visual design begins.

Deliverable: Wireframes & user flows
06
Phase 3 · Design

UI Design

High-fidelity, on-brand designs with typography, colour, imagery and motion, for desktop and mobile.

Deliverable: Figma designs & prototype
07
Phase 3 · Design

Design Approval

You review the designs, request revisions and approve the final look before development starts.

Deliverable: Signed-off design
08
Phase 4 · Build

Development & Coding

Clean, scalable code brings the approved design to life: front-end, back-end, database and admin panel.

Deliverable: Staging link with progress updates
09
Phase 4 · Build

Testing & Quality Assurance

Cross-browser, mobile, speed, security and form testing to make sure everything works flawlessly.

Deliverable: QA report & fixes
10
Phase 5 · Launch

Domain & Hosting Setup

We register or connect your domain, configure hosting, SSL certificate and business email if required.

Deliverable: Domain, hosting & SSL live
11
Phase 5 · Launch

Deployment & Launch

Your project goes live with on-page SEO, analytics and Google Search Console set up.

Deliverable: Live website / app
12
Phase 6 · Grow

Training, Support & Maintenance

We train you to manage your content, then provide ongoing support, updates, backups and improvements.

Deliverable: Handover guide & support plan
